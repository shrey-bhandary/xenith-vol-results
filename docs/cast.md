## Ricco, the builder and events sub-head

He is the only cursor that is not a department. He is the one who made the site,
and the build says so without a credit line. He is also sub-head of events, so
his frame pass can start on the events side of the page and finish on the frame.

- Colour: cream and gold `#ffe9b0` / `#ffb15c`, a 1px gold outline no other cursor
  has.
- Shape: the standard arrow, but with a small pixel star at the tail.
- Tag: `ricco · built this` in gold, slightly larger than the dept tags.
- Trail: a gold 1px trail that fades slower than the others.
- Sound: a low 220Hz chime when he acts, not the tick the heads get.

His beats:

1. After the X cracks the void, Ricco's cursor arrives first, alone. He draws the
   outer frame of the page in one pass, then writes `xenith` in the top left.
2. Only then do the heads pour in and start their sections.
3. At the end, when the page is built, Ricco returns, draws a small signature
   under the name, `built by ricco`, and parks next to Rusty.
4. If the visitor hovers his cursor, a one-line tooltip appears: `made the site.
   still fixing the small stuff.`

He never speaks over Rusty. His only line is the signature.
