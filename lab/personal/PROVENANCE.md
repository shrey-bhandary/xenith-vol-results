below, then rewritten to the house voice. Two records (shreyas, shivam) were
appointed rather than selected, so there is no answer to lean on and their lines

This file records which field each line leans on, so a reviewer can check the

`slug, name, dept, role, line, tag` reaches `site/data.js`.

## Rules the copy follows

The contract is in `lab/copy/PERSONAL-LINES.md`: 55 to 120 characters, sentence
case, a full stop, no exclamation marks, no dashes, at most one joke and it
lands on Xenith, a noun the person chose, no compliment about character, and it
should read like a friend replying at midnight.

Tags are a second beat, not a summary of the line. They are drawn on the shared
card under the department.

## Two people with no source

Shreyas and Shivam were appointed, not selected from the form, so no application
answers exist for them. Their lines describe the job and claim nothing about
them. If either of them gets copy with a personal detail in it, that detail has
to come from a conversation, not from this file.
