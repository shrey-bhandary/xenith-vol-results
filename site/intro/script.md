# Xenith intro: six scenes

One action, honest loading, no chrome. A black void, a small robot, a pane of
glass, then the page. From zero we take the door, a count that is real, and the
refusal to decorate.

The authored clock is 22.00s from the void to the gate, 8.00s of gate, then the
existing page. The vendored engine takes the page at 30.95s.

Times are seconds from first paint. Copy in backticks is final. `none` is a beat
that is silent on purpose. All of Rusty's words are lowercase. There are no em
dashes in this script or in anything on screen.

## Frame

- void `#05040c`, amber `#ffb15c`, cream `#ffe9b0`, violet `#7b5cff`, mint `#9ef0c0`.
- Press Start 2P for Rusty and the two choices. System mono for the HUD, labels
  and the prompt. Rusty's screen is 8px PS2P on desktop, 6px on mobile, and
  never more than 8 characters.
- HUD: `xenith` left, `2026` right, one 8px word `sound` that reads `off` until
  the first gesture and then toggles the mix. Nothing else on screen.
- Honest loading: a 1px rule under the HUD fills in ten ticks, one per assembly
  step in scene 2, then disappears at 7.40. The only other progress is the mint
  on your own stroke.
- One Rusty. The menu's face icon is off; the console pads around his canvas.
  He is never rendered twice and he never teleports.
- The room does not drift. There is no room yet. The camera push is a 3 percent
  scale on Rusty from 3.00 to 22.00, and one 4px lean when the glass arrives.

## Handoff

The last frame of the intro and the first frame of the page are the same
composition. That is the contract. Everything below keeps that one frame true.

1. **30.00** `window.Xenith.unlock(false)`. The pad holds the strokes and has
   already run the burst. Unlock adds `entered` and `built`, fades `#gate` over
   900ms, prints the name, and starts the page's 950ms engine timer.
2. **The glass does not move.** The intro's frame edge and the pad's dashed guides
   share one geometry, so nothing scales, slides or redraws at 30.00.
3. **Rusty does not teleport.** He lets go at 29.60 and springs to his corner over
   950ms, shrinking to the page's sprite size on the way. `#rusty` takes the same
   pose at 30.55, with its usual 1.2s entrance overridden to 120ms, so the swap
   is invisible. The intro canvas is empty by 30.60, before the snapshot.
4. **30.95** `loadSwarmEngine()` runs exactly as today: `body.engine`, snapshot,
   blank, rebuild. Nothing of the intro is on screen when the snapshot is taken.
5. **One confetti.** It fires at 28.60 from the frame and dies at 30.80. The
   second one inside `finishUnlock()` does not fire on this path.
6. **One stamp.** `found.` is the stamp. `#selectStamp` stays off and the name
   print is the arrival.
7. **Sound.** The bed ducks at 30.00 and is out by 31.40. The engine's own sound
   waits for its toolbar button, as today.
8. **Escape** still calls `unlock(true)` and never loads the engine.
   `?entered=1`, `?engine=1` and `?gate=auto` still skip the intro.

Four lines outside this lab:

The console needs no new API. Hide `.xmenu__face` from the intro's sheet, pad
`.xmenu` to clear his canvas, and add the row ticks, the hover tick and the
select thunk to the intro's audio module. menu.js keeps the typing blips.

`body.intro` runs 0.00 to 30.00 and hides the page. The pad is inert until
22.00, then live. `body.intro-out` runs 30.00 to 31.40 and suppresses the stamp
and the second confetti.

## Rules

- **Voice.** Lowercase, short sentences, one joke per scene, no exclamation
  marks. He never explains a feature. He never names the engine.
- **One action per scene.** Two rows are one choice. The glass is one gesture.
- **Honest loading.** Nothing counts up that is not real. Ten ticks for ten
  parts. Mint for the distance you actually covered.
- **Never wait.** If no rows are picked by 15.20, `01` pulses and picks itself.
  If the glass stays untouched, the prompt pulses and `skip` brightens every 4s.
  The gate never times out.
- **Skip.** `skip` stays on screen from 0.00 and calls `unlock(true)`: the page,
  no engine. Escape does the same from any scene.
- **Pointer.** Only Rusty, the two rows and the glass answer it. No stage
  parallax, no tilt, no cursor follower before the gate.
- **Audio.** Silent until the first gesture. That gesture starts the bed, and the
  HUD word flips to `sound on`, which is also the mute. Cues before the gesture
  are skipped, not queued.
- **Reduced motion.** The six scenes at 60 percent, so the gate opens at 13.20.
  Rusty is static after boot, no confetti, no shake, no spin, success is a 120ms
  cream flash. The gate keeps its 8.00s.
- **Mobile.** `docs/mobile-intro.md` stands: U=4 at 375, frame at 62vw, rows
  stacked, 44px targets, 8px PS2P rows, bubble above the sprite.
